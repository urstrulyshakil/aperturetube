## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-end, cinematic dark environment engineered specifically for elite photographers and their selective clientele. Evoking the tactile luxury of precision optical engineering, mechanical Leica/Hasselblad chassis, and calibrated gallery environments, the design treats every client touchpoint as a museum-grade visual exhibition.

The design movement synthesizes **Luxury Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**:
- **Atmospheric Obsidian Canvas**: Deep, light-absorbing dark backgrounds prevent eye fatigue and provide an uncompromised neutral environment where high-dynamic-range photography pops without chromatic distortion.
- **Precision Metallic Gilt**: Gold is used with mechanical restraint, mirroring an optical aperture blade catching rim-lighting. It serves purely as focal emphasis, critical interactive cues, and tier-of-service verification rather than decorative excess.
- **Precision Optical Rigor**: Information density is balanced by deliberate micro-interactions, microscopic 1px glowing boundaries, and razor-sharp typographic hierarchy that honors the technical precision of professional camera systems.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an unyielding pitch-black foundation graduated across obsidian tones, overlaid with radiant brushed golds derived from high-precision mechanical brass and aperture iris blades:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37`)**: The authoritative metallic gold. Used for primary CTAs, active states, key navigation indicators, and branding emblems.
- **Secondary (`#F5D77F`)**: A pale champagne gold used for glowing highlights, hover accents, subtle illumination badges, and high-emphasis display headlines.
- **Tertiary (`#AA7C11`)**: Deep burnished antique bronze for sunken states, pressed button styles, and dark outline transitions.
- **Neutral & Surfaces (`#0B0C10`, `#14161D`, `#1C1F2B`)**: Pure black canvas foundation graduating into slate obsidian cards and elevated modal surfaces. These deep values ensure total visual isolation for displayed media.
- **Micro-Glow Accents (`border-gold-glow`)**: Transparent radial gold rims create subtle depth without harsh contrast lines.

## Typography

The type system balances geometric clarity with industrial utility. Headings leverage **Plus Jakarta Sans** for a contemporary, sculpted posture with tailored negative tracking that commands visual presence in titles and gallery names.

Body prose and operational UI utilize **Inter** to ensure micro-legibility at all scales, particularly when scanning metadata (f-stops, shutter speeds, ISO ranges, client delivery tokens). **JetBrains Mono** is introduced selectively for raw EXIF telemetry, cryptographic share hashes, storage quotas, and temporal timestamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8pt base grid implemented through a strict fluid container architecture:
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column grid with dynamic outer margins up to `3rem` and generous `1.5rem` gutters. Masonry and staggered proofing matrices allow edge-to-edge full-bleed preview modes.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters for streamlined proofing passes and side-by-side split inspectors.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column flow with condensed `1rem` side margins and `0.75rem` internal component gutters to maximize photo viewing surface area on pocket devices.

Vertical rhythm follows a breathing rhythm where metadata, controls, and administrative actions are compressed into crisp micro-clusters (`space-xs` to `space-md`), leaving major section expanses (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`) exclusively for visual gallery staging.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy operates through atmospheric luminance and translucent obsidian strata rather than traditional opaque drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Surface)**: Deep light-sink `#0B0C10` absorbing background glare.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation)**: `#14161D` with a subtle 1px border rendered in `border-subtle` (`#282C3D`).
- **Level 2 (Active Panels / Flyouts)**: `#1C1F2B` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an ambient shadow `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Hero Lightboxes)**: `#14161D` with a rim-lighting accent: `0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` accompanied by a multi-tier halo `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9), 0 0 32px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
- **Micro-Glow**: Selected interactive elements and focused active aperture dials produce a soft peripheral bloom using `rgba(245, 215, 127, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft** architectural silhouette (`roundedness: 1`). Elements favor precision geometric cuts over round or toy-like shapes, evoking optical lens barrels, precision-milled aluminum chassis, and classic darkroom slides:

- Standard controls, text inputs, buttons, and thumbnails employ a refined `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- Cards, modals, and preview tiles scale to `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Floating toolbars, photo inspection HUDs, and circular aperture badges retain strict geometric purity: either square-cut with subtle softening or complete mathematical circular pills for status chips and aperture iris emblems.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Gold**: Gradient fill running from `#F5D77F` down to `#D4AF37`, text in deep `#0B0C10` (weight: 600). Hover produces a luminous ambient golden aura (`0 0 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`). Active state depresses slightly with `#AA7C11`.
- **Secondary Dark**: Background `#1C1F2B`, hairline 1px border `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`, text in `#F4F5F7`. Hover increases border glow to full `#D4AF37`.
- **Ghost / Text**: Transparent background, text `#9CA3AF`, shifting to `#F5D77F` on hover with a subtle golden underline transition.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Semi-translucent obsidian fills (`rgba(28, 31, 43, 0.8)`) bounded by 1px hairline borders.
- Proofing status chips (e.g., *Selected*, *Delivered*, *In Retouch*) use dedicated accents: active proofing tagged with gold pulse indicators, approved assets tagged with `#10B981`.
- Text renders in `label-mono` uppercase with wide tracking for a technical calibration feel.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Base background `#0E1015` with borders set to `#282C3D`.
- Focus state activates an instantaneous crisp transition to `1px solid #D4AF37` and an inner soft bloom (`0 0 8px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`). Placeholder copy is muted at `#525866`.

### Photography Proofing & Workflow Cards
- Dark-slate bounding tiles (`#14161D`) featuring 1px corner highlights.
- Image previews default to full bleed with a dimming scrim on hover that reveals micro-actions: Favorite (heart toggle), Flag for Edit, EXIF Inspector, and Quick Download.
- Hover triggers a smooth 1px rim illumination using `border-gold-glow`.

### Floating HUD & Aperture Iris Indicator
- Detached photo inspection toolbars float above full-bleed imagery with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` over `rgba(11, 12, 16, 0.85)`.
- Features rotary aperture blades icon as a vector mask that opens/closes dynamically according to selected f-stop values or delivery lock states.
